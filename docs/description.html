# RGX | Better Level-Up! ## ![](https://github.com/RGXMods/SimpleQuestPlates/blob/main/media/kiwi.gif?raw=true) RGX Mods - RealmGX Community Project *** ## 🎯 Overview **RGX | Better Level-Up!** replaces repetitive World of Warcraft sounds with memorable audio from more than 50 games — levels, achievements, quests, reputation, battle pets, delves, honor, housing, and Trading Post milestones all get their own customizable cue. BLU is the Retail edition. Classic players should install [BLU Classic](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/blu-classic), and WoW Forever players should install [BLU Forever](https://github.com/RGXMods/BLU_Forever). ![RealmGX Kiwi](https://github.com/RGXMods/SimpleQuestPlates/blob/main/media/kiwi.gif?raw=true) **The Kiwi Says:** "Ding! But make it a victory fanfare! Bwwiiiee." *** ## ✨ Features 🎵 50+ Game Sound Libraries - Favorites from Final Fantasy, Zelda, Mario, Skyrim, Pokemon, Warcraft III, and many more. 🏆 Extensive Event Coverage - Levels, achievements, quests, reputation, renown, battle pets, delves, honor, housing, and Trading Post activity. 🔊 Granular Audio Control - Per-event choices, Low/Medium/High volume variants, previews, and selective muting of matching WoW sounds. 📦 Sound Packs - Automatic discovery plus public APIs for simple and full three-volume third-party packs. 🗂️ Custom Sounds - Register your own .ogg, .mp3, or .wav files in game. ⚙️ Modern Configuration - Tabbed options, profiles, modular event features, and built-in diagnostics. **Note:** Each bundled selection includes Low, Medium, and High variants. Special collections may include several alternate cues. *** ## 🎮 Supported Events Achievements | Achievement earned and criteria progress Battle Pets | Pet level-up and pet capture Combat | Combat start, combat end, combat music, and supported proc cues Delves | Companion level-up, life lost, and life gained Honor | Honor rank increases Housing | Favor/XP, level-up, rewards, and decor collection Leveling | Character level-up Quests | Accepted, turned in, complete-at-quest-giver, and objective progress Reputation | Standing and major-faction renown rank increases Trading Post | Activity completion and purchases Event availability follows the current Retail client. Unused event groups can stay disabled. *** ## 📋 Slash Commands The addon can be managed using the following slash commands:
/blu # Opens the options panel
/blu help # Displays help information
/blu status # Shows addon status
/blu enable | disable # Enables or disables BLU
/blu debug # Toggles diagnostics
/blu refresh # Rebuilds external and custom sound registries
/blu rescan # Discovers newly registered media
/blu addcustom myfile # Adds a compatible custom sound
/blu removecustom path # Removes a registered custom sound
*** ## 🎵 Sound System Bundled sounds - Full Low, Medium, and High variants included with BLU. External sound packs - Addons discovered automatically or registered through BLU's public sound-pack APIs BLU:RegisterExternalSoundPack() and BLU:RegisterSoundPack(). See the [Sounds Guidelines](https://github.com/RGXMods/BLU/wiki/Sounds-Guidelines) for layouts and examples. User custom sounds - Add compatible files from the Sounds tab or with /blu addcustom. Run /blu refresh after adding or removing files and /blu rescan after another addon registers media. ![RealmGX Kiwi](https://github.com/RGXMods/SimpleQuestPlates/blob/main/media/kiwi.gif?raw=true) **The Kiwi Says:** "Preview before you commit — your ears will thank you! For the WoW Forever build with Forever-only tuning use [BLU Forever](https://github.com/RGXMods/BLU_Forever). *** ## 📥 Installation 1. **Download** RGX | BLU from your preferred platform: * [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/betterlevelup) * [Wago.io](https://addons.wago.io/addons/blu) * [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/RGXMods/BLU/releases) 2. **Install** the required [RGX-Framework](https://github.com/RGXMods/RGX-Framework) dependency if your addon manager does not resolve it automatically. 3. **Extract** to your WoW directory: * **Retail WoW:** World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns 4. **Restart** WoW and enable both addons in the AddOns menu, then type /blu.

*** ## ⚙️ Configuration Open /blu and use the tabbed options panel to configure event modules, profiles, sounds, and diagnostics. Settings are stored in BLUDB, and profiles keep separate setups without duplicate addon folders. *** ## 🔧 Troubleshooting * Confirm WoW's Master volume is enabled. * Use /blu rescan after another addon registers new media. * Use /blu refresh after adding or removing external or custom packs. * Some audio may pause briefly the first time WoW caches it. * Confirm BLU and RGX-Framework are both enabled if /blu does not open. *** ## 💝 Support the Project * **☕️ Buy Me a Coffee:** [Donate](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/donniedice) * **💸 Donate via CashApp:** [Donate](https://bit.ly/3fyxxSU) * **⭐️ Star on GitHub:** [BLU](https://github.com/RGXMods/BLU) * **📢 Share:** Share it with friends and guildmates! *** ## 🤝 Feedback and Contributions * **🐛 Report Issues:** [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/RGXMods/BLU/issues) * **🔧 Contribute:** Bug reports, translations, feature ideas, and sound suggestions are welcome via [GitHub](https://github.com/RGXMods/BLU). * **📞 Contact:** Join the [RealmGX Discord](https://discord.gg/N7kdKAHVVF) for help, feedback, and sound suggestions. *** ## 📄 License This project is licensed under the [MIT License](https://github.com/RGXMods/BLU/blob/main/LICENSE). *** ## ⚠️ Disclaimer **Disclaimer:** This BLU addon is provided "as is" without warranty.
