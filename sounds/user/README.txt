User custom sounds

Put your own .ogg, .mp3 or .wav files in this folder, then fully restart WoW (a /reload does not pick up new files).
Name them custom01 to custom99 and they appear in the sound lists on their own; any other name is added from
/blu > Sounds > Add Custom Sound, or with /blu addcustom <name>.

BLU updates replace this whole addon folder, this folder included. Keep a copy of your sounds, or put them in
Interface\AddOns\BLU_Sounds\ instead (a folder next to BLU that updates never touch). BLU looks in both.
